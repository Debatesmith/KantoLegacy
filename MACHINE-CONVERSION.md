# Machine conversion

Exact taught-move matches take priority, regardless of TM number. Otherwise,
these explicit substitutions apply. They are converter compatibility choices,
not official equivalents or identical effects. Outputs are native FireRed items.

| Razor Wind, Swift, Sky Attack | TM40 Aerial Ace |
| Swords Dance | TM08 Bulk Up |

All other base RBY machines retain their taught move. Unsupported modded items
are audited separately. Many-to-one quantities add. PC machines enter the TM
Case. Historical pickup/reward flags never grant consumable stock. HM story
grants remain because field access is durable and HMs are reusable.

Reimport RBY to apply this mapping; older LEGACY inventories are unchanged.
